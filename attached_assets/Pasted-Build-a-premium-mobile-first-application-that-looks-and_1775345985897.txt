Build a premium mobile-first application that looks and behaves like a fully native iPhone app.

App Name: Esteticar
Category: Car Wash Booking Platform

CORE IDEA:
This is NOT a ride-sharing app.
This is a smart booking system for car wash services across multiple physical locations.

Users book a time slot at a nearby car wash location, arrive at the scheduled time, and receive service without waiting in line.

No real-time GPS tracking, no driver movement, no Uber-like logic.
